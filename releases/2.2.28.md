**Released:** 2026-09-26 · **Channel:** stable · **Critical:** no

Turkish: [2.2.28-tr.md](2.2.28-tr.md)

## Forum shortcuts in the user menu

- The dark shortcut bar under forum pages (Unread, My drafts, My topics, Messages, Mark all read) is removed.
- While browsing the forum, the header user menu opens in two columns: the usual menu on the left, and a Forum column with Unread, My topics, My drafts and Mark all read (or Mark board read on a board) on the right.
- Outside the forum, for example on the blog, the menu is unchanged.
- Messages stays in the menu's left column only.

No schema changes.
